# Why this path, and why you don't need to panic

_Facts as of September 2026, with sources. Read this whenever the layoff headlines get to you, then go do your next lesson. Most solid data comes from the US; the course started in India, so section 2 focuses there. Data for your own country is a welcome [contribution](../CONTRIBUTING.md)._

## The honest picture

### 1. The door into routine entry-level coding has narrowed
- **Stanford Digital Economy Lab, August 2026 update** (US payroll data to June 2026): employment of 22–25-year-olds in the most AI-exposed jobs is about **19% below** where it would be if it had kept pace with less-exposed peers. The gap comes from **fewer hires, not more layoffs**, and experienced workers show no gap. Where AI **complements** work instead of automating it, young workers' employment is flat or rising. The authors present these as descriptive facts, not proof that AI caused them. ([report](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2026/08/Canaries_August2026.pdf))
- **Indeed Hiring Lab, July 2026** (US): only **4.5%** of software postings in Q1 2026 were entry-level, and senior roles made up 71% of the rebound in postings. ([Indeed](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/07/08/ai-and-job-postings-from-destruction-to-creation/))
- **Harvard study, October 2025:** at firms adopting generative AI, junior employment fell about 9–10% after six quarters while senior employment did not change. ([paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5425555))

### 2. There is no mass displacement: entry is harder, not closed
The same Stanford data finds no economy-wide displacement, and US software postings have been rising again since early 2025, with jobs that have "AI" in the title driving much of the growth ([Indeed](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/07/08/ai-and-job-postings-from-destruction-to-creation/)).

**Focus: India.** IT services still hire freshers, with a higher bar:
- **TCS** announced about 12,000 job cuts in July 2025, aimed mainly at middle and senior management, yet onboarded about 44,000 trainees in FY26 and about 14,000 freshers in Q1 FY27. ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/ai-overhaul-indias-tcs-to-cut-12000-jobs-this-year-after-growth-slows-in-q1-1.500212931), [StartupFeed](https://startupfeed.in/tcs-adds-9279-jobs-q1-fy27/))
- **NASSCOM, February 2026:** about 6 million tech employees, but net headcount grew only about 2.3%. Firms need fewer people per rupee of revenue, so **each person has to be worth more**. ([NASSCOM](https://nasscom.in/knowledge-center/publications/technology-sector-india-strategic-review-2026))
- **Naukri JobSpeak:** AI/ML job postings were **up 31%** year on year in August 2026, and fresher hiring rose too. ([LatestLY/ANI](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/business-news-aiml-hiring-rises-31-pc-yoy-in-august-gcc-recruitment-grows-10-pc-naukri-jobspeak-7594785.html))
- **TeamLease EdTech, H2 2026:** IT employers expect "AI fluency, applied problem-solving, and hands-on project experience from day one", and **"AI Application Developer" is the most in-demand fresher role**. ([Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/hans/education-careers/fresher-hiring-outlook-improves-to-75-in-hy2-2026-teamlease-edtech-report-finds-1108625))

### 3. Demand for people who build with AI is growing fast
- **LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise 2026:** AI Engineer is the **#1** fastest-growing job in the US and **#2 in India**. ([US](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/linkedin-jobs-rise-2026-25-fastest-growing-roles-us-linkedin-news-dlb1c), [India](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/linkedin-jobs-rise-2026-25-fastest-growing-india-jrtnc))
- **PwC Global AI Jobs Barometer 2026:** a **62% wage premium** for AI skills. Entry-level roles that AI makes *more demanding* grew 35% since 2019, while other entry-level roles fell 10%. ([PwC](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ai-reshapes-global-labour-market-into-two-distinct-paths-rewarding-human-skills-pwc-2026-global-ai-jobs-barometer-302798987.html))
- **World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs 2025:** 170 million jobs created and 92 million displaced by 2030, a net gain of 78 million. AI/ML specialists are among the fastest-growing roles, and analytical thinking is the top skill employers want. ([WEF](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Future_of_Jobs_Report_2025.pdf))

### 4. AI makes developers faster, but it can make learners weaker
- **GitHub Copilot lab test (2023):** developers finished a coding task **55.8% faster**. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590))
- **Field trials with 4,867 developers** at Microsoft, Accenture and a Fortune 100 firm: **26% more tasks** completed, and **juniors gained the most** (27–39% vs 8–13% for seniors). ([paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4945566))
- **METR, July 2025:** experienced open-source developers were **19% slower** with AI tools while believing they were 20% faster. Feelings are not measurements. ([METR](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/))
- **Anthropic, January 2026:** junior engineers learning a new library with AI scored **50% vs 67%** on a comprehension quiz, with the biggest gap in **debugging**. Learners who used AI to *ask conceptual questions* kept their learning (65%+); those who *delegated the work* fell below 40%. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills))

That last study is why your Mentor gives hints instead of answers.

## Seven habits that keep you valuable (the course enforces them)
1. **Understand before you delegate.** Use AI to ask "why", not to skip the thinking.
2. **Keep some work AI-free on purpose.** Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index found the highest-impact professionals do this more often than others (43% vs 30%). ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization))
3. **Always verify.** Read, run, test and explain AI-generated code before you accept it. 66% of developers complain AI output is "almost right, but not quite". ([Stack Overflow 2025](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai))
4. **Measure, don't trust feelings.** Time yourself, count defects, build eval sets.
5. **Build in public.** Projects on GitHub, short posts about what you built. Employers ask for hands-on projects.
6. **Learn your company's domain.** AI plus domain knowledge solves real problems; AI alone doesn't.
7. **Become the AI person on your team.** Demos, lunch-and-learns, helping colleagues. Teaching also deepens your own understanding.

## What nobody knows yet
- **Causality is not proven.** Interest rates and post-COVID over-hiring also explain the junior slowdown. Some economists find weak or no AI effect so far ([EIG](https://eig.org/ai-and-jobs-the-final-word/), [Yale Budget Lab](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/evaluating-impact-ai-labor-market-current-state-affairs)), and the slowdown may turn out to be temporary.
- **There is no causal study for India yet.**
- **Specific tool skills expire fast; fundamentals don't.** That's why this course spends months on math, ML and how models work, and not only on frameworks.
