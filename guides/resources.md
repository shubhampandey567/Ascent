# The library: best free resources, by topic

The phase files tell you *what to study when*. This page is the shelf you come back to. Everything here is free to use (a few certificates cost money; noted). Checked September 2026.

## If you only remember ten
1. [3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown): visual intuition for linear algebra, calculus, neural networks and transformers.
2. [StatQuest](https://www.youtube.com/@statquest): statistics and ML explained step by step with real numbers.
3. [Andrej Karpathy](https://www.youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy): build neural networks and GPTs from scratch; the best LLM explainers.
4. [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn): short hands-on courses (Python, Pandas, ML, SQL…) in the browser, free certificates. Plus free GPUs.
5. [Google Machine Learning Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course): concise, modern ML fundamentals.
6. [fast.ai — Practical Deep Learning for Coders](https://course.fast.ai/): top-down deep learning on free Kaggle GPUs.
7. [Hugging Face Learn](https://huggingface.co/learn): LLM, Agents, MCP and Context courses with free certificates.
8. [DeepLearning.AI short courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/): videos free to watch on almost every GenAI topic (quizzes and certificates are paid "Pro").
9. [Microsoft "for Beginners" curricula](https://github.com/microsoft): [ML](https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners), [Generative AI](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners), [AI Agents](https://github.com/microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners), [MCP](https://github.com/microsoft/mcp-for-beginners).
10. [Hamel Husain's writing on evals](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals/): the skill that separates AI engineers from demo builders.

## Certificates worth having (free or nearly)
- Kaggle Learn certificates (free).
- Hugging Face course certificates: Agents, MCP, Context, LLM (free).
- DataTalksClub Zoomcamp certificates (free, but only in a live cohort with projects).
- In India: NPTEL (IIT) certificates (exam fee ~₹1,000; recognised by Indian employers).
- Coursera certificates: paid, but **Financial Aid** is available per course (apply about 2–3 weeks ahead; don't start a free trial while an application is pending).

Certificates open doors a little; projects and how you talk about them open them a lot.

## Staying current
- Newsletters: [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/) (weekly, broad), [Ahead of AI](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/) (research, clearly explained).
- Papers: [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers) (Papers with Code shut down in 2025).
- Blogs: [Anthropic engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering), [Chip Huyen](https://huyenchip.com/), [Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/), [Lilian Weng](https://lilianweng.github.io/).

## How to judge a new resource
Before starting any course not listed here, ask the Mentor, or check yourself:
1. Is it free all the way through, or only the first module?
2. When was it last updated? LLM material older than about a year may use dead models or APIs.
3. Does it make you *do* things (exercises, projects), or only watch?
4. Does it fit your current phase, or is it a shiny distraction?
