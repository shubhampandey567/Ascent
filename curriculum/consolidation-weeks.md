# Consolidation weeks

**Plan weeks 13, 28, 44 and 60 · no new content**

These weeks come after Phases 2, 4, 6 and 8. They repair forgetting, strengthen weak spots, and turn projects into portfolio pieces. Spacing and relearning research says this is some of the most valuable time in the course, even though it feels like "not moving forward". Lesson ids are `C<week>-L<n>`, for example `C28-L2`.

## The week

#### C<week>-L1 · Clear the backlog (~60 min)
Run `/recall` until every due topic is done (still at most 8 per sitting; do two sittings if needed). Then go through every open row in `tracker/weak-spots.md` and answer it without notes.

#### C<week>-L2 · Mixed test (~60 min)
The Mentor asks 15 questions across **all** phases so far, weighted towards the topics with the lowest ease in `python tools/srs.py list`. No notes. Record each topic's score with `python tools/tracker.py review <ID> <score>`.

#### C<week>-L3 · Relearn the two weakest topics (~75 min)
Pick the two lowest-scoring topics from L2. For each, use a *different* resource than the first time (the lesson's "Alt" link or a regional-language alternate), write a fresh note from memory, and re-quiz.

#### C<week>-L4 · Polish a project (~75 min)
Open the weakest project's `REVIEW.md` and fix its "top 3 improvements". Improve the README so a stranger could understand it in 2 minutes: problem, demo, results, how to run.

#### C<week>-B · Weekend: learn in public, then rest
Write one short post (LinkedIn, dev.to or Hashnode) about something you built in this block: what you made, one thing that broke, what you learned. Run `/weekly`. Then take a real day off.
